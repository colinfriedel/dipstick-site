What it does

01

Scan the pump

Point the camera at the pump display and the gallons and total fill in.

02

Real city and highway MPG

Drives record on their own when your phone connects to the car, split into city and highway miles.

03

Maintenance that keeps up

A schedule for your car, service records with parts and receipts, and reminders when things are due.

04

Bring your history

Import past service records from photos, a Carfax report, a PDF or a spreadsheet.

Private by design

No accounts, no ads, no tracking. Your records stay on your iPhone, and drives keep only city and highway miles, never your route. Read the privacy policy.
